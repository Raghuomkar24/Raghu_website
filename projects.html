    🔗 Vocal Link

    Converting sign language into text and audio in real-time, it provides a powerful tool for individuals with hearing impairments to communicate seamlessly in both physical and digital spaces. With this app, they can engage in social media, participate in conversations, and express themselves just as easily as those without disabilities, breaking down the barriers that limit their ability to connect and be heard.

    • Enhanced Communication: Enables seamless communication between people who are deaf or hard of hearing and those who do not know sign language. Helps bridge the gap in communication in various settings (e.g., healthcare, education, customer service)
    • Real-Time Recognition: Real-time processing allows for immediate feedback and interpretation, making it practical for live interactions
    • Non-Contact Technology: Image-based sign language detection does not require physical contact, making it hygienic and suitable for various environments.
    • Scalable and Adaptable: The system can be easily scaled to include multiple sign languages or can be trained with more diverse datasets to handle different gestures or signs
